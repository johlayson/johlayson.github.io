The pale blue dot

In 1990, as Voyager 1 was heading out of the solar system, Carl Sagan asked NASA to turn its camera around one last time. From about six billion kilometers away, it took a picture of home. Earth was a single speck of light, smaller than a pixel.

“Look again at that dot. That's here. That's home. That's us.”

Carl Sagan, Pale Blue Dot

Someone was curious enough to look back. That's the kind of curiosity I want to keep.
